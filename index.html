📊 Interpretation Guide

K-Index:
<15: No storms
15-20: Isolated
21-30: Scattered
31-35: Numerous
>35: Severe
Lifted/Showalter Index:
>2: Stable
0 to 2: Marginal
-2 to 0: Moderate
-6 to -2: Unstable
<-6: Very unstable
